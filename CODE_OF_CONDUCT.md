# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive, specific technical feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/nagendhra-web/New-Repo/security/advisories/new)
(select "conduct" in the description) or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports are
reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior violates this
code, following the enforcement ladder in the Contributor Covenant (correction, warning,
temporary ban, permanent ban).
